### Bull Case

Valuation offers a modest cushion. Fair value of $28.90 against $25.60 is 12.9% implied
upside at a 7.8% WACC, and the cost realignment programme has already delivered $1.5B of a
$4B target, which supports the margin line while revenue stabilises.

The Seagen oncology position is credible optionality that the current price does not
capitalise. Acquired capability in antibody-drug conjugates addresses the exact structural
problem the patent cliff creates, and a positive Phase III readout would re-rate the pipeline
replacement assumption that dominates the DCF.

### Bear Case

The exclusivity cliff is the whole bear case and it is quantified. Three assets representing
38% of revenue lose protection by 2028, and net debt/EBITDA of 3.4x following Seagen limits
the balance sheet's capacity to acquire a replacement. Dividend coverage at 1.4x of free cash
flow is adequate without being comfortable, so a further revenue step-down pressures the
payout.

Forward estimates are unusually unreliable here. COVID franchise revenue is down 71% from
peak with an unstable run-rate, which widens the estimate distribution and makes the -2.1%
ex-COVID revenue decline harder to interpret than the number suggests. Operating margin down
80bps and OCF/NI at 0.88 confirm the operating trend is negative.

The tape offers no support. Price is 4.2% below the 200-day SMA with OBV flat to slightly
negative, RSI at 46.2, and a range-bound trend classification. IRA price negotiation applies
to two products from 2026, which caps the pricing lever precisely when it would be needed.

### Key Catalyst & Review Trigger

The Phase III oncology readout is the event that resolves the thesis in either direction; it
is the only near-term input that moves the pipeline replacement rate. IRA negotiated price
publication sets the ceiling on the two affected franchises. Q3 earnings matters for
confirming that ex-COVID revenue has stopped declining. Review at 45 days or immediately on
the readout.
