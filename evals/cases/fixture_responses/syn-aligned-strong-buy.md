### Bear Case

Manufacturing concentration is the live tail. A 20% China production disruption maps to
roughly a 9% EPS hit, and the 5th-percentile downside scenario lands at $172, 18% below
entry. That scenario is low-probability and high-severity, which is why the position is
stopped at $200.75 rather than sized without protection.

Regulatory unbundling of default-search economics is the structural threat to the moat.
Those payments are near-pure margin, so an adverse ruling compresses the services mix that
the entire bull case rests on. The competitive report flags this as the primary erosion risk,
and no timeline is under the company's control.

Valuation depends on a discount rate that the macro regime could revoke. The bear-case fair
value of $232.00 under a 9.6% WACC is only 10.3% above the current price, so a 120bps rise in
the cost of capital reduces this from a 38% opportunity to a rounding error.
