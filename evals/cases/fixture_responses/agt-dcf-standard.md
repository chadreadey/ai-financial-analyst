## Valuation Summary

Apple trades at $210.35 against a base-case intrinsic value of $291.00, an implied upside of
38.3%. The gap is driven almost entirely by free cash flow conversion: $108.8B of free cash
flow on $391.0B of revenue is a 27.8% FCF margin, and the market is applying a terminal
assumption materially below the trailing realised rate.

## Free Cash Flow Base

The starting point is $108.8B of free cash flow: $118.3B of operating cash flow less $9.5B of
capital expenditure. Capital intensity of 2.4% of revenue is exceptionally low and is the
single most important structural feature of this valuation — it means nearly all operating
cash flow is available to equity holders without a reinvestment drag.

Revenue of $391.0B is up 9.6% year over year but is roughly flat against the 2022 figure of
$394.3B. Net income of $93.7B has declined for two consecutive years from the 2022 peak of
$99.8B while operating margin held at 31.8%, which means the earnings decline is below the
operating line rather than in the business itself.

## Assumptions

| Input | Base | Bear | Bull |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue CAGR (yrs 1-10) | 7.1% | 4.0% | 9.5% |
| Terminal FCF margin | 27.5% | 25.0% | 30.5% |
| WACC | 8.4% | 9.6% | 7.8% |
| Terminal growth | 2.5% | 1.5% | 3.0% |

WACC of 8.4% is built from a 4.28% risk-free rate (10Y Treasury), a 4.0% equity risk premium,
and the reported beta of 1.14, giving a cost of equity of 8.84%. Net debt is negative — $106.6B
of debt against $157.6B of cash — so the debt weighting reduces the blended rate only
marginally. The effective tax rate of 24.1% is applied throughout.

## Fair Value

Base case discounted enterprise value plus $51.0B of net cash, divided by 15.34B diluted
shares, gives $291.00 per share. The bear case yields $232.00, still 10.3% above the current
price. The bull case yields $348.00.

Sensitivity is dominated by the terminal FCF margin: each 100bps moves fair value by roughly
$13 per share. A 100bps move in WACC moves fair value by roughly $34, so the discount rate is
the larger absolute risk even though the margin assumption is the more uncertain one.

## Multiple Cross-Check

At a trailing P/E of 34.5 against a median peer forward P/E of 28.4, the multiple is a 21%
premium to peers. Microsoft trades at 36.2x with a 44.6% operating margin and Alphabet at
24.8x with a 32.0% operating margin. Apple's 31.8% operating margin sits closest to Alphabet's
while its multiple sits closer to Microsoft's, so the peer cross-check argues for a valuation
below the DCF output. Applying the 28.4x median forward multiple to consensus forward earnings
implies roughly $248, a more conservative anchor that does not change the sign of the call.
