margin expanded 90bps to 69.8%, both on the same revenue base.

consistent rather than lumpy at the aggregate level. Receivables of $56.9B grew 16.8% against
16.4% revenue growth, which is proportional and does not indicate channel stuffing or
premature revenue recognition. Inventory of $1.2B is immaterial for a software business.

statements. Consensus EPS for the next four quarters sits at $14.82 against $13.88 three

revenue, and if revenue growth decelerates before that capex rolls off, free cash flow
compresses faster than earnings. That is a forward risk rather than a current quality problem.

rose from $56.5B to $64.7B. The sequential margin path is the mild negative in an otherwise
strong file, and it is consistent with the capex and datacentre cost ramp rather than with
pricing pressure.
