1. REVENUE MODEL & RECURRING MIX: Evaluate the proportion of recurring vs. one-time revenue. For SaaS/cloud, assess ARR growth, net retention rate, and gross margin trajectory. For hardware, analyze replacement cycles and attach rates.

5. COMPETITIVE MOATS: Analyze platform effects, developer ecosystem lock-in, data advantages, and switching costs. Identify the key threat vectors (open-source substitution, hyperscaler vertical integration, regulatory action).
