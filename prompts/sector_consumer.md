4. CONSUMER HEALTH & DEMAND SIGNALS: Consider same-store sales trends, volume vs. price growth decomposition, consumer sentiment indicators, and trade-down risk in the current macro environment.

5. COMPETITIVE LANDSCAPE: Evaluate market share trends, category growth rates, innovation pipeline (new products, formats), and M&A consolidation dynamics in the category.

Be quantitative where data permits. This is a sector briefing, not a full investment report -- keep it focused and actionable.
